Organisation-specific brand and content governance profile

Purpose. A flexible, source-backed structure for policies extracted from an organisation's uploaded repository. It is not a mandatory checklist: sections and attributes appear only when supported by source material or confirmed by an authorised reviewer.

Critical rule. Integrix AI must not invent a policy to fill an empty field. Missing, ambiguous, conflicting and not-applicable information must have distinct statuses.

Template design rules for Integrix AI

  • Maintain a stable common schema for organisation identity, policy records, provenance, verification and approval.
  • Allow optional sections and flexible domain-specific metadata; do not require every organisation to populate every field.
  • Keep missing, ambiguous, conflicting and not-applicable states distinct.
  • Preserve source provenance to page, sheet, cell range or image region where possible.
  • Separate extraction confidence, policy severity and human approval.
  • Keep draft policies inactive until an authorised reviewer approves them.
  • Version profiles and retain prior decisions so audits can be reproduced against the policy version active at the time.
  • Treat examples in this template as illustrative; actual rules must come from the organisation's repository or explicit confirmation.
