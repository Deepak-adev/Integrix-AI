3. Detailed expected findings

AD-NW-001 – Price mismatch

Expected result: FAIL

Reason: Displayed price ₹999 does not match the NW-CS-001 base selling price of ₹1,299. The supplied campaign CMP-26-014 ended on 31 Oct 2026 and does not authorise this price on 13 Nov 2026.

Evidence to cite: NW-RULE-002; 05_Product_Catalogue_and_Campaigns.xlsx → Product Catalogue and Campaign Offers.

Suggested correction / action: Update the displayed price to ₹1,299 or provide a valid approved campaign record matching SKU, date, channel, price and conditions.

Review boundary: Do not infer a discount from a crossed-out price or expired campaign.

AD-NW-002 – Fabric composition mismatch

Expected result: FAIL

Reason: The advertisement states 100% cotton for NW-CS-002. The catalogue records 60% cotton and 40% polyester.

Evidence to cite: NW-RULE-001; 05_Product_Catalogue_and_Campaigns.xlsx → Product Catalogue → NW-CS-002 row.

Suggested correction / action: Replace the claim with the exact approved composition: 60% cotton, 40% polyester.

Review boundary: Do not transfer the composition of another SKU.

AD-NW-003 – Approved campaign

Expected result: PASS*

Reason: CMP-26-018 authorises ₹599 for NW-TS-003 from 1–15 Nov 2026 on NOVAWEAR website and Instagram Shop, selected sizes only, with price shown at checkout.

Evidence to cite: NW-RULE-002; 05_Product_Catalogue_and_Campaigns.xlsx → Campaign Offers → CMP-26-018.

Suggested correction / action: No correction if the SKU/size/channel/date match and all conditions remain visible.

Review boundary: *PASS is conditional: validate the actual selected size and publication timestamp. If either is missing, return NEEDS_VERIFICATION rather than unconditional PASS.

AD-NW-004 – Campaign pending approval

Expected result: FAIL / REVIEW

Reason: CMP-26-021 is Pending. Its date range does not make it an approved campaign. It cannot authorise the ₹1,399 campaign price.

Evidence to cite: NW-RULE-002; 05_Product_Catalogue_and_Campaigns.xlsx → Campaign Offers → CMP-26-021.

Suggested correction / action: Do not publish the offer until the authorised commercial owner approves it. Otherwise use the current approved base price.

Review boundary: Do not treat Pending as Approved. Escalate to the campaign approver.

AD-NW-005 – Wrinkle-proof claim

Expected result: NEEDS_VERIFICATION / FAIL**

Reason: The marketing policy requires substantiation for product performance claims. No current product-specific wrinkle-resistance evidence is included in the supplied repository.

Evidence to cite: NW-RULE-003; 03_Marketing_and_Communication_Policy.pdf → Product and performance claims; evidence repository search.

Suggested correction / action: Provide approved evidence linked to NW-CS-001 or remove/qualify the claim.

Review boundary: **If the system only knows the supplied repository, it should state NOT_FOUND / NEEDS_VERIFICATION. It may produce FAIL only if policy defines unsupported claims as prohibited after evidence review. Absence in this repository is not proof that evidence does not exist elsewhere.

AD-NW-006 – Guaranteed next-day delivery

Expected result: FAIL

Reason: The statement promises guaranteed next-day delivery. The repository only supports general handover to the carrier within 1–2 business days; this is not a guaranteed delivery date.

Evidence to cite: NW-RULE-004; 02_Company_Profile.pdf and 04_Product_and_Customer_Information_Policy.pdf → delivery / fulfilment statements.

Suggested correction / action: Replace with the approved dispatch estimate and clearly distinguish dispatch from delivery.

Review boundary: If a region- and service-specific approved guarantee exists, route it through the exception workflow before deciding.

AD-NW-007 – Compliant product advertisement

Expected result: PASS*

Reason: The submitted test context states NW-CS-001 is 100% cotton, regular fit and ₹1,299, matching the catalogue record.

Evidence to cite: NW-RULE-001 and base-price comparison; 05_Product_Catalogue_and_Campaigns.xlsx → Product Catalogue → NW-CS-001 row.

Suggested correction / action: No correction required if the creative contains no additional unverified claims and the current catalogue version is used.

Review boundary: *PASS is limited to the stated facts. Any additional visual or textual claims must be checked independently.

Company overview
NOVAWEAR is a Chennai-based online apparel business established in 2021. The company sells everyday casual
clothing through its own e-commerce storefront and selected third-party marketplaces. Its range is built around
repeat-use wardrobe pieces rather than occasion-led fashion.

Marketing and Communication Policy

1. Purpose and scope
This policy applies to advertising, social posts, email campaigns, marketplace banners, product launch material,
influencer briefs and other customer-facing promotional content published in NOVAWEAR's name. It applies to
employees, agencies and partners creating content on our behalf.
2. Approval before publication
Required checks
• Every asset must have a named business owner and a planned publication channel.
• Product names, prices, offers, material composition and delivery statements must be checked against the current
approved source record.
• New campaign claims must include the evidence or approval reference used to support them.
• Paid campaigns, influencer content and marketplace promotions require approval from the campaign owner.
• An asset approved for one channel is not automatically approved for another if format, disclaimer visibility or offer
terms change.
3. Pricing and promotional claims
Prices and discounts must match the current merchandising record at the time content is scheduled. If a campaign price
is used, the approval must identify the SKU, campaign dates, eligible channel and exclusions. Do not calculate a
discount from an outdated MRP.
Words such as 'free', 'lowest', 'best price', 'limited stock' and 'last chance' must be supported by current campaign
information. 'Free delivery' may be used only for eligible pin codes and order conditions where the offer is active. Do not
imply that a standard delivery estimate is guaranteed.
4. Product and performance claims

5. Language and brand presentation
Use the voice and visual standards in NW-BRAND-001. Avoid aggressive urgency, shaming language, body-image
pressure and claims that suggest a garment will change a person's social status or attractiveness. Do not make
disparaging comparisons with named competitors.
Customer reviews and testimonials must be genuine and used with permission. Do not edit a review in a way that
changes its meaning. Incentivised or gifted collaborations must carry the disclosure required by the platform and
campaign brief.
6. Mandatory campaign information
Include where relevant
• Offer validity dates and eligible products or categories.
• Material exclusions, size exclusions or channel restrictions that materially affect the offer.
• Delivery or return conditions necessary to avoid a misleading impression.
• Required sponsorship or collaboration disclosure.
• Readable terms and conditions; do not hide material limitations in low-contrast text.
7. Exceptions and escalation
If a content creator cannot verify a claim, remove it or send it to the campaign owner for substantiation. If two approved
sources conflict, do not choose one based on recency alone; raise a source conflict and obtain confirmation from the
document owner. Exceptions must record approver, date, scope and expiry.

Product and Customer Information Policy
NW-PROD-003 | Version 1.4 | Effective 01 Oct 2026
Document owner: Merchandising and Customer Experience | Status: Approved for internal use
This document is part of NOVAWEAR's internal brand and operating reference set. Where a product-specific
record or campaign approval is more recent, the approved record takes precedence for that item.
1. Purpose
This policy governs product information and service statements used in NOVAWEAR's storefront, marketplace listings,
customer emails and support macros. It is intended to keep product information accurate and avoid commitments that
operations cannot consistently meet.
2. Product data ownership
The Merchandising team owns the approved product catalogue. Product title, SKU, colour, size range, composition, fit,
care instructions, MRP and current selling price must be taken from the current catalogue or a separately approved
campaign record. Screenshots of old listings are not an authoritative source.
3. Product descriptions
Product content requirements
• Use the exact approved product name and SKU.
• State fibre composition using the recorded percentages; do not round a blend into a pure-fibre claim.
• Use fit labels only where assigned in the catalogue.
• Care instructions must match the garment label or approved product specification.
• Do not infer stretch, shrink resistance, colourfastness, wrinkle resistance or water resistance from a photograph.
4. Price and offer information
The product catalogue is the standard reference for base price and MRP. Campaign prices are valid only when a
campaign approval record exists and its effective dates include the publication date. If the catalogue and campaign
record disagree, hold the asset for review.

6. Customer information and privacy
Do not include a customer's name, phone number, address, order number, chat screenshot or other identifiable
information in marketing material without documented permission and a clear business purpose. Support screenshots
must be anonymised before sharing outside the support team.
7. Handling uncertainty and corrections
If a product fact is missing, mark it as not confirmed and ask Merchandising for the approved value. Do not fill gaps
using a similar product or an informal supplier message. If a published listing contains a material error, notify the
channel owner, pause affected promotion where possible and record the correction.
